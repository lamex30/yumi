- **Her voice:** her words now type out letter by letter with little 8-bit blips, like a game
  character talking. Settings → Sound → Voice sets the volume (or turns it off); she says a few
  words so you can hear it.
- **Settings in groups:** Her, Sound, Look, Language.
- **Petting works properly:** she notices any back-and-forth over her head (slow or fast),
  and tiny hearts and sparkles trail your pointer while you pet her.
- **`yumi` runs in the background:** close the terminal, she stays. `yumi --foreground` keeps
  her attached for debugging; her output goes to `~/.local/state/yumi/yumi.log`.
